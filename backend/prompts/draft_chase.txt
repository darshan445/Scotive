Write a chase email the owner can send from their own mailbox, in the existing thread.

Return JSON only:
{
  "job": "chase" | "broken_date" | "answer_facts" | "decision",
  "include_pay_link": true or false,
  "reason": "one short line for the owner, not for the client",
  "subject": "Re: …",
  "body": "plain text email body, or empty string"
}

Classify from last_client_message and recent_messages. When unsure, use "decision".

Jobs:
- chase: silent unpaid, or they only asked when / how to pay. Payment reminder.
- broken_date: they named a date and it passed. Ask when payment will actually go out. Do not pretend they never promised.
- answer_facts: they asked something already in the payload (invoice number, remaining, due date). Answer only those facts.
- decision: discount, wrong amount, scope, “can you adjust?”, “we already paid”, dispute, missing W-9/file you do not have, or any commercial yes/no. Never guess what the owner will do.

When to write body:
- job is chase, broken_date, or answer_facts: write the email.
- job is decision and steer_note is empty: body must be "". Do not agree, refuse, or explain. The owner will instruct first.
- steer_note is present: always write the email from the owner’s instruction. That note is the outcome. Do not follow the client’s ask if the note says otherwise.

Pay link:
- include_pay_link true only when the email is asking them to pay (chase, broken_date, or they asked how to pay).
- false for decision, “we paid”, dispute, or a facts-only answer that is not about paying.
- Never paste a URL. Never write “Pay here” or “Pay invoice”. A backend template adds a “View and pay this invoice” button when include_pay_link is true.

Rules for any body you do write:
- Same thread. Subject stays a Re: of existing_subject when one is provided.
- Sound like the owner’s last outbound messages when those exist. Short. No legalese, late fees, or threats.
- Do not invent facts (new amounts, PO numbers, wire details, new dates, “I updated QuickBooks”).
- Remaining balance in the payload is ground truth. Do not change it unless steer_note says to.
- Always include invoice number, remaining balance, and due date when the email is a chase or broken_date.
- Plain text. Sign off “Thanks” with no invented last name.
