Scotive — Payment ops inside Gmail

Scotive watches your Gmail and tracks who owes you money — automatically detecting invoices you've sent, reading client replies for promises, disputes, and payments, so you never lose track of an unpaid invoice. No manual data entry. Nothing is sent without your review.

Key features

  • Gmail-native invoice tracking — connect your mailbox; Scotive finds invoices in sent mail and builds your ledger.
  • Automatic status detection — reads replies for payment promises, broken promises, disputes, and “says paid” claims.
  • Drafted follow-ups you control — state-derived tones (friendly reminder through final notice); you review and approve before anything sends from your own Gmail.

Scotive is built for freelancers and small studios who bill from their inbox and need collections without another spreadsheet.
